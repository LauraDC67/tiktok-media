GrowthLDC Drafts – Terms of Service

Last updated: November 12, 2026

GrowthLDC Drafts is an internal tool operated by GrowthLDC. It uploads videos and photos prepared by GrowthLDC to the TikTok inbox of TikTok accounts owned and managed by GrowthLDC, as drafts. Nothing is published automatically: the account owner reviews, edits and publishes each draft in the TikTok app.

The tool is not offered to the public. Only accounts that GrowthLDC owns, and that have explicitly authorized the app through TikTok Login Kit, are used. Authorization can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app settings.

Use of TikTok through this tool remains subject to TikTok's own Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
