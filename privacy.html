GrowthLDC Drafts – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

GrowthLDC Drafts is an internal tool used only with TikTok accounts owned by GrowthLDC.

Data we access

With the account owner's authorization, the tool receives the account's TikTok open ID, display name and access tokens (scopes user.info.basic and video.upload). It uses them only to upload GrowthLDC's own videos and photos to that account's TikTok inbox as drafts.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored locally on GrowthLDC's computer and are never shared or sold. Photos are hosted publicly for a few minutes, only long enough for TikTok to download them, then deleted. No data from TikTok users other than the authorized accounts is collected.

Revoking access

The account owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services). To have stored tokens deleted, contact laura@rocapine.com.
